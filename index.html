A look inside

For grown-ups

A grown-ups' corner sits behind a parent gate, so a child cannot wander in. From there you can:

  • See what they are learningEvery skill in plain words, with its level out of ten, what comes next, and the age level they are playing at overall.
  • Follow their weekWhich games they played, day by day, and what they finished.
  • Set their age, if you likeThe game finds the right level within a few rounds; an age starts each skill at that age's level.
  • Turn things downSeparate volume for music, sound effects and the narrator's voice.
  • Start learning afreshReset the learning progress, in two confirmed steps; their friends and coins stay.
  • Unlock or restoreBuy the full game once, or restore it on another device.

Always getting better

Tumbleberry Town keeps growing. We watch how children play and listen to the families who tell us what is confusing or missing, then fix it: clearer pictures, better sounds, new things to wear and collect, and new rooms. Everyone who has unlocked the game gets every update, free. You pay once.

Try it free, then unlock once

Nine games (the first in every room), a free-play toy in every room and all of Home Base are free. One payment unlocks all 38 games and every future room, and every update after it. No subscription, no in-game shop for real money, and nothing to buy again.

Coins and eggs are earned only by playing, never sold. Purchases, links and settings all sit behind the parent gate.
