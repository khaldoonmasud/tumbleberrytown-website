Grown-ups' corner

How do I open the grown-ups' corner?

Tap Grown-ups at the top right of the town map. A parent gate asks you to type a number written in words (for "four hundred and sixty-two", type 462). The Unlock all button on the map and in Home Base opens the same gate, then the unlock. Inside you will find your child's progress, this week's play, settings (sound and graphics), age, the unlock, reset, and the app's details.

What do the levels mean?

Each skill (counting, letters, shapes and so on) has ten levels. The game moves up when your child gets things right and quietly back down when something is hard. The Progress tab says, in plain words, what your child is learning now and what comes next.

Does the game need my child's age?

No. Every game starts gently and adjusts to how your child plays within a few rounds. If you like, set an age in the grown-ups' corner under Age to start each game at that age's level.

How do I start again?

Grown-ups' corner, then Reset. There are two choices, and each asks twice before doing anything:

  • Reset progress clears the learning progress. Coins, eggs, clothes and friends are kept.
  • Start over erases everything on the device, as if the game were new: coins, friends, clothes, the room, stickers and progress. The unlock and the sound settings are kept. Useful when the device passes to another child.

Buying and restoring

What is free, and what does the unlock add?

Eight games (the first in every room), every room's toy and all of Home Base are free. One purchase unlocks all 34 games and every future room. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy: coins and eggs are earned by playing and can never be bought.

I bought the unlock but the games are locked

Open the grown-ups' corner, go to Unlock and tap Restore purchase. The game asks your store (App Store, Google Play or Amazon) and unlocks everything if the purchase is there. Use the same store account you bought it with. If a payment is still pending (for example waiting for a parent's approval), the games open by themselves once it goes through.

Does the unlock work on my other devices?

Yes, on any device signed in to the same store account: install the game, then use Restore purchase. Progress itself stays on each device, because the game keeps nothing online.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com), Google Play (your order history in the Play Store) or Amazon (Your Apps and Devices). We are happy to help if you write to us.

Playing

My child cannot read yet. Is that all right?

Yes. Every instruction is spoken, and Say again repeats it. If your child waits a few seconds, a pointing hand shows what to do.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tumbleberry Town works completely offline, with no ads and no tracking. See our privacy policy.

Can my child change how their character looks?

Yes, any time and for free: Home Base, then the Dress-Up Shop, then My look (the button with their character's face). They can choose a skin tone, a hair colour and a hairstyle.

The sound is too loud or too quiet

Grown-ups' corner, then Settings: music, sound effects and the voice each have their own volume.

Troubleshooting

The game is slow or jerky, or the device gets warm

Grown-ups' corner, then Settings, and turn on Simpler graphics. It shows fewer moving friends and effects and asks less of the device. It helps most on older or lower-cost tablets. Closing other apps helps too.

There is no sound

Check the device's own volume, and on an iPhone or iPad the silent switch or silent mode. Check that headphones or a speaker are not still connected. Then, in the grown-ups' corner under Settings, make sure music, sound effects and voice are turned up.

The game does not open, or closes by itself

Restart the device, and make sure it has a little free storage, since the game needs some space to save progress. If it still will not open, reinstall it (below). If the problem stays, please write to us: the grown-ups' corner, under About, shows the app version and your device's details to include.

Clearing the game's data or reinstalling it

This erases the progress on that device, so try the steps above first.

  • Android phones and tablets: Settings, then Apps, then Tumbleberry Town, then Storage, then Clear data. Or uninstall it and install it again from Google Play.
  • Amazon Fire tablets: Settings, then Apps & Notifications, then Tumbleberry Town, then Storage, then Clear data. Or uninstall it and install it again from the Amazon Appstore.
  • iPhone and iPad: touch and hold the app's icon, then Remove App, then Delete App, and install it again from the App Store. ("Offload App" keeps the progress and does not reset it.)

If you bought the unlock, it comes back: open the grown-ups' corner, then Unlock, then Restore purchase, using the same store account.

Still stuck?

Email support@tumbleberrytown.com with what happened and the details shown in the grown-ups' corner under About (the app version and your device). Please don't send any details about your child.
