Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Tumbleberry Town is a learning game for children aged 3 to 7. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about your child, and it does not.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
  • The game works completely offline. It does not connect to the internet.
  • Your child's progress is saved only on your device.

What the game keeps, and where

To remember where your child is, the game saves a small file on the device: the age you chose in setup (3, 4, 5, 6 or 7+), the cartoon character your child picked, the learning level for each skill, which games have been played and on which day (for the weekly summary in the grown-ups' corner), coins, eggs, stickers, creatures, clothes, garden and room decorations, sound settings and whether the full version is unlocked.

This file never leaves the device. We cannot see it. It contains no name, photo, email address, location, contact details or device identifier. Deleting the app deletes it. "Reset progress" in the grown-ups' corner clears the learning progress.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through the App Store, Google Play or the Amazon Appstore. The store handles the payment and its own records under its own privacy policy. The game only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought, so that it can open the games, and "Restore purchase" asks the store again. We never receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not need, and does not ask for, parental consent to collect data. This is how we meet children's privacy laws such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

Purchases, outside links (this page and our support email) and the grown-ups' settings are behind a parent gate, so a child cannot reach them by accident.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking code. The only outside code is each store's own purchase system, used only for the unlock.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its date. We will never start collecting personal information from children.
